Data Privacy & Architecture

ScrollShield adheres to a zero-telemetry, offline-first philosophy:

  • All configurations and metrics are stored strictly on your device via Chrome's local storage.
  • No external servers, tracking scripts, third-party analytics, or remote API endpoints.
  • Minimal permissions: only the "storage" permission is requested to persist your focus preferences.
